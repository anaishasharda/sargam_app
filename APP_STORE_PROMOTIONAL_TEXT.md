## 📝 Description (Up to 4000 characters)

**Riti - Your Gateway to Indian Classical Music**

Transform your passion for music into mastery with Riti, the most comprehensive app for learning Indian classical music. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, Riti guides you through the enchanting world of ragas with cutting-edge technology and traditional wisdom.

**🎵 Learn Authentic Ragas**
Master timeless ragas like Bhoopali, Bhairav, and Bhairavi through structured lessons designed for every skill level. Each raga comes with detailed explanations, audio demonstrations, and practice exercises that help you understand the essence of Indian classical music.

**📊 Real-Time Audio Analysis**
Experience the power of instant feedback with our advanced audio analysis technology. As you sing or play, Riti analyzes your pitch, tempo, volume, and note accuracy in real-time, helping you improve with every practice session.

**🎯 Personalized Learning Paths**
Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced levels tailored to your experience. Each level features carefully curated ragas that build upon your existing knowledge, ensuring steady and meaningful progress.

**📈 Track Your Progress**
Set practice goals, maintain a musical journal, and visualize your improvement over time. Watch as your skills evolve with detailed analytics and insights into your practice patterns.

**🎨 Beautiful, Intuitive Design**
Enjoy a modern, elegant interface that makes learning a joy. Smooth animations, beautiful visualizations, and thoughtful design create an immersive experience that keeps you engaged.

**✨ Key Features:**
• Learn multiple ragas with audio demonstrations
• Real-time pitch and note detection
• Sargam notation support (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni)
• Practice session tracking and analytics
• Musical journal for reflections
• Goal setting and achievement tracking
• Beautiful audio visualizations
• Progress monitoring with detailed insights

**🎼 Perfect For:**
• Music students learning Indian classical music
• Vocalists and instrumentalists
• Music teachers and educators
• Anyone passionate about Indian classical traditions
• Beginners starting their musical journey

Start your journey into the rich heritage of Indian classical music today. Download Riti and let the melodies guide you to mastery.
